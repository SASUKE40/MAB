Reference

How a bandit agent decides

Each step the agent picks one of K arms and sees only that arm's reward. It never sees the means, so it cannot know what it lost. Regret counts that loss from the outside.

Simulation core: src/mab.js; add-ons in src/algos/ and src/settings/. In the K-armed arena, regret is pseudo-regret, Σ (μ*ₜ − μaₜ); for drifting and abrupt environments μ*ₜ is the best mean at step t. Bonuses are scaled by s = 1 for Bernoulli and s = √2 for unit-variance Gaussian rewards.
